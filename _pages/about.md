I am a PhD student at [Distrinet](https://distrinet.cs.kuleuven.be/), doing research on Network Security under the supervision of [Mathy Vanhoef](https://www.mathyvanhoef.com/).

My research primarily focuses on network security. More specifically, I research the security of tunnelling protocols, as well as possible off-path,on-path, and network-adjacent attacks.
